Amik

Privacy policy

Last updated 20 September 2026.

Amik is an offline habit tracker with a private journal, published by Hym. It has no account and no server. What you write stays on your phone. The one thing Amik ever sends is a crash report, and a crash report carries nothing you wrote.

What Amik keeps, and where

Your habits and their schedules, whether each one was done, the daily totals, your goals, your journal entries with their moods and answers, anything you have started writing and not saved, and your settings are stored in a database inside the app on your phone.

They are not sent anywhere, and the developer has no way to read them. Removing Amik from the phone deletes all of it.

What leaves your phone

One thing: a crash report, sent when Amik crashes.

  • It goes to Sentry, a crash reporting service run by Functional Software, Inc., and is stored in the European Union.
  • It carries the error and the place in Amik's own code where it happened, the version of Amik, the phone's model and maker, the Android version, how much memory the phone has, and the language and time zone the phone is set to.
  • It never carries a habit name, a journal answer, a mood, a draft, or anything else you typed or chose. Amik rebuilds every report from a fixed list of fields before sending it, leaving everything else out, and automated tests check this on every change to the app.
  • Sentry is told not to store the network address the report was sent from.
  • Crash reports are deleted automatically after 90 days at most.

There is no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no profile and no artificial intelligence in Amik. It makes no other network request.

Android's own backup

If backup is switched on in your Android settings, Android may copy the data of the apps on your phone, Amik included, into your Google account, as it does for other apps. That copy is made by Android, governed by Google's terms and your device settings, and neither Amik nor its developer can read it. You can switch it off in the phone's settings.

Your data stays yours

Nothing you record in Amik leaves the phone unless you send it somewhere yourself. Amik asks for no personal information, no contacts, no location and no photos.

Children

Amik is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If the policy changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top says when. A change that affects what leaves your phone will be described here plainly.
